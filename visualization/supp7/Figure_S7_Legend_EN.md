**Supplementary Figure S7. Calibration of statistical tests under the observed study designs.**

Calibration was conditional on the observed participants, covariates, assay batches and follow-up patterns. Counts refer to protein analyses or model comparisons. Pathology and relative-response simulations included 34 study proteins and MAPT as a separate reference. Qualification counts also incorporate the prespecified component-test and numerical-failure criteria in Supplementary Methods Appendix B; the relative-response row reports evaluation at the nominal significance level only.

(A) Markers show the largest empirical null rejection rate in each analysis group; caps show the largest cell-specific Wilson 95% upper bound. Nominal testing used α=0.05, with a prespecified upper-bound limit of 0.06. Draws per design/error-structure cell were 40,000 for shared p-tau181 analyses, 100,000 for cognitive analyses, 10,000 for fourteen-protein sensitivities, 60,000 for adjustment comparisons and 4,000 for relative-response confirmation. The first seven rows used five error structures; relative responses used six. These bounds are cellwise; the displayed maxima are not simultaneous 95% bounds.

(B) Each point represents one of the 291 designs with an evaluated small-tail criterion. Its horizontal value is the largest ratio of a Wilson upper bound to the applicable BY first-step rejection threshold, over the tested null means, error structures and numbers of comparisons. Values ≤1 meet that criterion; rose marks CST3 memory, which did not qualify. Vertical separation within a row only reveals overlapping designs. Evaluated numbers of comparisons were 34 and 68 for pathology, 68, 136 and 204 for cognitive analyses, 28 for clinical sensitivities, and 9, 33 and 42 for adjustment comparisons. The fixed BY first-step cutoffs define sufficient thresholds for rejection.

(C) The two CST3 memory tests are shown separately. The denominator is 0.06 for the nominal check and the original BY first-step threshold for each tail check; the dashed line denotes a ratio of one. Only the shared-direction upper bound at the 204-test threshold exceeded its limit. The corresponding worst null cell had one rejection in 100,000 draws: the empirical rate was below the threshold, but its Wilson upper bound, approximately 0.0000566, exceeded the required 0.0000416. This failed the prespecified design-level rule. CST3 memory inference was withheld and correction inputs remained P=1; formal inference was therefore unavailable.

(D) The relative-response simulations had insufficient resolution to assess the smallest BY threshold. With zero rejections among 4,000 draws, the Wilson upper bound was approximately 0.000959, exceeding the 34-test threshold of 0.000357. The panel compares these quantities to show why that threshold could not be assessed with the available simulation count.

Null mean patterns were specified separately for same-direction and between-assay-difference tests. Noise draws were reused across mean patterns. Calibration applies to the examined scenarios; full criteria and results are provided in Supplementary Table S9 and Supplementary Methods Appendix B. Power is presented in Supplementary Fig. S10.

Abbreviations: APOE, apolipoprotein E gene; BY, Benjamini–Yekutieli.
