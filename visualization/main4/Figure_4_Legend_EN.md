**Figure 4. p-tau181 adjustment and between-assay differences in memory associations.**

Exploratory analyses evaluated NPTX2 (n=553), TAFA5 (n=557) and TIMP3 (n=552), selected from the preceding memory comparisons. Paired models used the same participants, cognitive assessments, weights, covariates and protein scales.

(A) Open and filled symbols show protein–memory coefficients before and after adjustment for linear log₂ p-tau181. Color and shape identify assays; arrows connect the two estimates. Units are annual ADNI-MEM change per fixed assay-specific protein residual SD.

(B) Changes in the SomaScan minus NULISA and TMT-MS minus NULISA contrasts, calculated as adjusted minus unadjusted. Bars show pointwise 95% confidence intervals that account for covariance across assays, paired models and protein scales. Joint two-degree-of-freedom tests compared the two changes, with BY correction across nine comparisons. Alternative p-tau181 forms and the combined 42-test sensitivity analysis are presented in Supplementary Fig. S12.

(C) Pairwise residual correlations after adjustment for the common covariates. Colored edges show assay-specific protein correlations; the p-tau181–memory correlation is common across assays within each protein. Solid and dashed lines denote positive and negative correlations. Line width represents the absolute correlation coefficient.

(D) Models including all three background scores. Solid bars test between-assay differences in the p-tau181-adjusted model; hatched bars test changes after adding p-tau181 with the background scores held constant. Both tests were included among the 33 additional comparisons. Heights represent −log₁₀q, labels show q, and the dashed line marks q=0.05. NPTX2 retained a between-assay difference (q=0.0228); its additional adjustment-related change did not meet the corrected threshold (q=0.237).

All models included participant-specific fixed intercepts and time interactions with demographic, genetic and technical covariates. The comparisons were exploratory, with correction for the specified comparisons following candidate selection. Background scores summarize broad variation in relative protein measurements. Details are provided in Supplementary Tables S4, S14–S17 and Supplementary Figs. S11–S13.

Abbreviations: ADNI-MEM, ADNI memory composite; BY, Benjamini–Yekutieli; CI, confidence interval; CSF, cerebrospinal fluid; p-tau181, tau phosphorylated at threonine 181; SD, standard deviation; TMT-MS, tandem mass tag mass spectrometry.
