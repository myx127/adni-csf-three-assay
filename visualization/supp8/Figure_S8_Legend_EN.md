**Supplementary Figure S8. CSF protein associations with baseline memory and annual executive function change.**

The figure shows all 34 proteins in the primary analysis, ordered alphabetically. Each protein was evaluated using identical participants across the three assays. Participant counts were the same for the two secondary outcomes and ranged from 342 to 557, as shown in the n column. Baseline memory was measured by ADNI-MEM at the valid assessment closest to CSF collection within ±90 days. Executive function change was estimated from baseline and eligible ADNI-EF follow-up assessments within five years after CSF collection. Actual follow-up characteristics are described in Supplementary Fig. S3.

(A) Circles, squares and diamonds show the NULISA, SomaScan and TMT-MS associations with baseline memory, respectively. Horizontal bars denote pointwise 95% confidence intervals (CIs). Coefficients are ADNI-MEM score differences per one fixed assay-specific protein residual standard deviation (SD), without a yearly denominator. Linear regressions adjusted for age, sex, education, APOE ε4 carrier status, standardized log₂ CSF p-tau181, all three proteomic technical factors and ADNI Elecsys data release. Positive coefficients indicate higher baseline memory scores.

(B) Symbols and CIs are defined as in Panel A, but coefficients represent annual ADNI-EF score change per one fixed protein residual SD. Longitudinal models included participant-specific fixed intercepts and time interactions with the covariates listed for Panel A. Positive coefficients indicate more favorable annual executive function change, including less decline. The two outcome panels use different horizontal scales because their units differ. Protein SDs were estimated without p-tau181 or cognitive scores in the scaling regression and held constant across outcomes. CIs account for coefficient and protein-scaling uncertainty using covariance contributions from the three proteomic technical-cluster axes. The gray dashed lines denote zero association;

(C) BY-adjusted q values for same-direction associations and between-assay differences for both outcomes. Values are rounded to three significant figures; color shows −log₁₀q and rose borders mark q≤0.05. Correction included all 136 secondary comparisons: 34 proteins, two outcomes and two tests. The intersection–union test considered both directions using unscaled coefficients; the paired two-degree-of-freedom Wald test jointly evaluated standardized SomaScan minus NULISA and TMT-MS minus NULISA contrasts. Models retained paired estimates and protein-scale uncertainty.

No same-direction association met the secondary corrected threshold. NPTX2 showed between-assay differences in executive function change (q=0.0206). Bold protein names and pale green shading identify the 14 proteins with shared p-tau181 associations in Supplementary Fig. S4.

Calibration qualification was evaluated separately for each protein–outcome design. All 68 designs shown here, including both CST3 designs, met the calibration criteria. Further details and full results are provided in Supplementary Methods Sections 3, 4 and 6 and Appendix A, Supplementary Tables S10–S11, and the supplementary data.

Abbreviations: ADNI-EF, Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative executive function composite; ADNI-MEM, ADNI memory composite; APOE, apolipoprotein E gene; BY, Benjamini–Yekutieli; CI, confidence interval; CSF, cerebrospinal fluid; SD, standard deviation; TMT-MS, tandem mass tag mass spectrometry.
