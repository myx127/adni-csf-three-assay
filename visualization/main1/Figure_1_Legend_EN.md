**Figure 1. Study design and analytical framework.**

(1) Paired ADNI CSF cohort. The study included 571 participants with NULISA, SomaScan and TMT-MS measurements matched by participant and CSF collection date. The earliest matched visit was included for each participant. Baseline cognition was assessed within 90 days of CSF collection; follow-up assessments occurred more than 90 days after collection and were included up to five years after collection.

(2) Three assay technologies. NULISA uses antibody recognition and nucleic acid reporters, SomaScan uses modified DNA aptamers, and TMT-MS quantifies labeled peptides summarized at the protein level. Apricot, green and blue identify the assays, respectively.

(3) Paired protein comparisons. Thirty-four proteins met the matching and quality criteria; MAPT was evaluated separately. Each protein–outcome comparison used the same participants across assays. Protein residual standard deviations provided comparable association units and were held fixed for cognitive comparisons.

(4) Shared p-tau181 associations. p-tau181 was measured using a separate Elecsys assay. A joint test evaluated whether the protein association was positive in all three assays or negative in all three, allowing for both possible directions. Multiple-testing correction included all 34 proteins.

(5) Cognitive associations. Annual ADNI-MEM change was the primary outcome; baseline ADNI-MEM and annual ADNI-EF change were secondary outcomes. All 34 proteins were evaluated for associations in the same direction across assays and for differences in association strength.

(6) Exploratory analyses. Candidates identified by the memory comparisons were examined before and after p-tau181 adjustment. Paired tests evaluated changes in assay differences using the same participants, cognitive assessments, covariates and protein scales. Background adjustment, sensitivity analyses and simulations examined the interpretation and statistical performance of these comparisons.

The schematic separates shared pathological associations, cognitive associations and changes in assay differences after pathology adjustment. Participant selection and detailed methods are presented in Supplementary Fig. S1 and Supplementary Methods.

Abbreviations: ADNI, Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative; ADNI-EF, ADNI executive function composite; ADNI-MEM, ADNI memory composite; CSF, cerebrospinal fluid; p-tau181, tau phosphorylated at threonine 181; SD, standard deviation; TMT-MS, tandem mass tag mass spectrometry.
