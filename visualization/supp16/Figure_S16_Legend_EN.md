**Supplementary Figure S16. Targeted peptide associations with p-tau181 and technical repeatability for NPTX1 and KLK6.**

(A) Positions of the five targeted peptides in canonical NPTX1 (Q15818; 432 amino acids) and KLK6 (Q92876; 244 amino acids), drawn on the same amino-acid scale. Teal and lavender indicate the annotated pentraxin domain (residues 226–428) and peptidase S1 domain (22–242), respectively; rose segments indicate the measured peptide sequences. Residue positions refer to the canonical UniProt sequences. All five peptides were gene-specific under exact and isoleucine/leucine-equivalent matching against the human reference proteome, including isoforms.

(B) Peptide associations with CSF p-tau181 in 95 ADNI participants with no overlap with the 571-participant paired three-assay sample. Points and bars show coefficients β and two-sided 95% confidence intervals. Effects are expressed as normalized peptide-signal change per one global SD of log₂ p-tau181. Each peptide signal is the mean of its two normalized log-transition measurements, without an additional logarithm. Models adjusted for age, sex, MRM plate, run, instrument and ADNI Elecsys data release. Variance combined plate and run CR1 components, with a Student t reference of four degrees of freedom. Displayed BY q values retain all five previously specified positive-direction tests; none met q ≤ 0.05. The positive-direction tests and two-sided intervals are defined in Supplementary Methods Section 9.

(C) Within-release technical repeatability for the 2014 and 2019 targeted-MS data, indicated by peach circles and blue squares. Points show Pearson correlations, and bars show 95% percentile intervals from 4,000 paired bootstrap resamples. The adjacent n values count complete replicate pairs: 16 for every peptide in 2014, and 19–20 in 2019. All interval limits are retained, including the negative lower limit for the 2014 ESSQEQSSVVR estimate. Measurements are on the normalized log-peptide scale. Concordance coefficients and additional repeatability diagnostics are reported in Table S19 and the accompanying data.

(D) Variation in raw light/heavy peak-area ratios across 24 pooled quality-control measurements in the 2019 dataset. Each point represents a fragment-ion transition, with two transitions per peptide; b and y identify the fragment-ion series. Coefficients of variation use ratios before normalization. All ten transitions had 24 usable positive ratios with no quality flags. These pooled-control measurements assess technical precision separately from the participant replicate correlations in Panel C.

These complementary peptide measurements were obtained within ADNI. The 95-participant association sample included 53 participants also measured in the 2014 dataset. Technical repeats and pooled controls assessed precision. Additional pathology analyses using the 2014 dataset required specimen-date and paired-design information that was unavailable. Details are provided in Supplementary Tables S18–S19 and Supplementary Methods Section 9.

Abbreviations: ADNI, Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative; BY, Benjamini–Yekutieli; CI, confidence interval; CV, coefficient of variation; MRM, multiple-reaction monitoring; PTX, pentraxin; SD, standard deviation.
