**Figure 5. Measurement context and peptide-level evidence for interpreting assay differences.**

(A) Weighted residual protein variation retained after p-tau181 adjustment alone (outer arcs) and after additionally including all three assay background scores (inner arcs). Percentages use the model with common covariates alone as the reference. Participants, cognitive observations, weights and protein scales were held constant within each candidate. Center labels give inner-arc percentages. The measure describes remaining variation in the observed protein measurements.

(B) An illustrative population model in which the assays measure one common signal with equal loadings and independent additive errors. Rows and columns vary the reference and comparison error variances. The matrix shows the change in the comparison-minus-reference association contrast after adjustment for a p-tau181 analogue, expressed per fixed preadjustment assay SD in synthetic outcome units. Full model equations and assigned parameters are provided in Supplementary Methods Appendix D.3.

(C) Canonical NPTX1 (Q15818; 432 amino acids) and KLK6 (Q92876; 244 amino acids) maps on a common residue scale. The annotated pentraxin and peptidase S1 domains span residues 226–428 and 22–242, respectively. Rose segments mark the five measured peptides. Searches of the human reference proteome, including isoforms and isoleucine/leucine-equivalent matching, supported gene-specific sequence assignments.

(D) Peptide associations with p-tau181 in 95 additional ADNI participants outside the three-assay sample. Points and bars show coefficients and two-sided pointwise 95% confidence intervals, in normalized log-peptide signal units per global SD higher log₂ p-tau181. Models adjusted for age, sex, plate, run, instrument and ADNI Elecsys data release. Covariance accounted for plate and run clustering, using a Student t reference with four degrees of freedom. BY q values correspond to the five positive-direction tests; none met q≤0.05.

These measurements concern NPTX1 and KLK6 within ADNI. Full results are provided in Supplementary Tables S16, S18, Supplementary Figs. S13, S15–S16, and Supplementary Methods Section 9 and Appendix D.3.

Abbreviations: ADNI, Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative; BY, Benjamini–Yekutieli; CI, confidence interval; p-tau181, tau phosphorylated at threonine 181; SD, standard deviation; TMT-MS, tandem mass tag mass spectrometry.
