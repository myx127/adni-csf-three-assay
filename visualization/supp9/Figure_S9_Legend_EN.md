**Supplementary Figure S9. Sensitivity of memory change associations among fourteen proteins with shared p-tau181 associations.**

All 14 proteins with shared p-tau181 associations in Supplementary Fig. S4 are shown alphabetically. The subset was defined by shared p-tau181 associations. Memory analyses used baseline and eligible ADNI-MEM follow-up assessments within five years after CSF collection; baseline could precede collection by up to 90 days. The n columns give protein-specific participant counts for each analysis.

(A) The primary model is repeated from Supplementary Fig. S6 as a reference. Models included participant-specific fixed intercepts and time interactions with age, sex, education, APOE ε4 carrier status, standardized log₂ CSF p-tau181, NULISA plate, SomaScan plate, TMT plex and ADNI Elecsys data release.

(B) The model omitted only the p-tau181 term, retaining the original complete-case participants, cognitive observations, other covariates and protein scaling. (C) Analysis was restricted to participants with at least two post-baseline follow-up assessments and at least two years from baseline to the last included assessment. This model retained p-tau181 adjustment. The restricted samples contained 366–410 participants, compared with 499–557 in the full protein-specific samples. (D) Each participant’s within-person memory slope received equal weight, rather than weighting by the observed within-person time information. This analysis retained p-tau181 adjustment and the original participants.

In Panels A–D, circles, squares and diamonds represent NULISA, SomaScan and TMT-MS, respectively. Coefficients are annual ADNI-MEM score change per one fixed assay-specific protein residual standard deviation (SD). All analyses use the same fixed protein SDs and horizontal scale. Positive coefficients indicate more favorable annual memory change, including less decline. Horizontal bars are pointwise 95% confidence intervals (CIs), incorporating coefficient and protein-SD estimation uncertainty through the three proteomic technical-cluster covariance contributions. Gray dashed lines denote zero association.

(E) BY-adjusted q values for same-direction associations (Shared) and between-assay differences (Diff.). The primary model retained correction across all 68 memory comparisons. Each sensitivity analysis separately corrected 28 comparisons: two tests for each of 14 proteins. Values are rounded to three significant figures; color indicates −log₁₀q and rose borders mark q≤0.05. The intersection–union test evaluated unscaled coefficients in both directions; the two-degree-of-freedom Wald test evaluated standardized SomaScan minus NULISA and TMT-MS minus NULISA contrasts together.

No same-direction memory association met the corrected threshold. Between-assay differences were significant for NPTX2, TAFA5 and TIMP3 in the primary and restricted-follow-up analyses; only NPTX2 remained significant with equal participant weighting, and none were significant without p-tau181 adjustment. Direct paired tests of adjustment-related changes are reported in Supplementary Table S15.

All 56 protein–analysis designs shown here met the calibration criteria. Full results are provided in Tables S7, S12 and the supplementary data; methods are described in Supplementary Methods Sections 4, 6 and 8 and Appendix A.

Abbreviations: ADNI-MEM, Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative memory composite; APOE, apolipoprotein E gene; BY, Benjamini–Yekutieli; CI, confidence interval; CSF, cerebrospinal fluid; SD, standard deviation; TMT-MS, tandem mass tag mass spectrometry.
