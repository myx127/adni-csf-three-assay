**Supplementary Figure S3. Cognitive follow-up in the analysis samples for individual proteins.**

Follow-up was characterized for all 34 proteins in the primary analysis. Each protein-specific sample required paired measurements from all three CSF assays, the clinical covariates used in the longitudinal models and eligible cognitive assessments. ADNI-MEM and ADNI-EF assessed memory and executive function. Baseline was the valid assessment closest to CSF collection within ±90 days. Follow-up assessments occurred after baseline, more than 90 days after collection and up to five years after collection.

(A) Bars show the number of participants in each protein-specific analysis sample, ordered alphabetically by protein. Counts were identical for memory and executive function and ranged from 342 to 557 participants.

(B) Circles and diamonds show the median time from baseline to the last included assessment for memory and executive function, respectively. Horizontal bars show the interquartile range (IQR), . Rows correspond to the proteins in Panel A. The span is measured from cognitive baseline and can slightly exceed five years when baseline precedes CSF collection.

(C) Each point represents the total number of included cognitive assessments in one protein-specific sample, including baseline. The median number of assessments per participant was five in every sample. Boxes summarize the 34 protein-specific totals for each cognitive domain: the central line denotes the median, the box the IQR, and the whiskers the most extreme values within 1.5 IQR. All points are shown, including those beyond the whiskers.

(D) Each point represents the percentage of participants in one protein-specific sample who met the indicated follow-up criterion. The first criterion required at least two post-baseline follow-up assessments and a baseline-to-last-assessment span of at least two years. The second required the last included assessment to occur at least 4.5 years after CSF collection. Percentages use the protein-specific participant counts in Panel A. The two follow-up criteria are displayed separately. Colors and symbols distinguish cognitive domains; boxes and whiskers are defined as in Panel C.

Descriptive summaries include all 34 proteins, including CST3, whose primary memory analysis did not meet calibration criteria. Details are provided in Supplementary Methods Sections 4 and 6, Supplementary Table S4 and the supplementary follow-up data.

Abbreviations: ADNI, Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative; ADNI-EF, ADNI executive function composite; ADNI-MEM, ADNI memory composite; CSF, cerebrospinal fluid; IQR, interquartile range.
