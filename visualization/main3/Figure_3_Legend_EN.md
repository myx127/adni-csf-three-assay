**Figure 3. Cognitive associations of CSF proteins across three assays.**

(A) Evidence for memory associations in the same direction across all three assays is plotted against evidence for differences between assays, using −log₁₀ of BY-adjusted q values. Each point represents a protein; diamonds identify proteins with shared p-tau181 associations. Dashed lines mark q=0.05. The 33 proteins meeting memory-analysis calibration criteria are shown; 24 proteins share the position q=1 for both tests. CST3 was excluded from memory inference, with its two hypotheses retained in the 68-test correction. NPTX2, TAFA5 and TIMP3 showed between-assay differences; no joint same-direction memory association met the corrected threshold.

(B) NPTX2 associations with baseline ADNI-MEM and annual ADNI-EF change in 553 participants. Color and shape identify assays. Horizontal and vertical bars show pointwise 95% confidence intervals for the respective outcomes. Axis units differ. Displayed q values compare the three assays, with correction across the 136 secondary comparisons.

(C) Annual memory associations for NPTX2, TAFA5 and TIMP3, expressed as ADNI-MEM units/year per fixed assay-specific protein residual SD. Bars show pointwise 95% confidence intervals, including protein-scale uncertainty. All plots use the same scale. Positive coefficients indicate more favorable memory change. Gray lines connect assay estimates. The q values compare the three coefficients jointly, with correction across 68 primary comparisons.

(D) BY-adjusted q values for between-assay differences under the primary and sensitivity analyses. Colors identify proteins. The dashed line marks q=0.05; the vertical axis is logarithmic. The four analyses included 68, 204, 28 and 28 comparisons, respectively. Restricted follow-up required at least two follow-up assessments spanning at least two years. TIMP3 did not meet the 204-test threshold; only NPTX2 retained significance with equal participant weighting.

Models adjusted for p-tau181, age, sex, education, APOE ε4 status, proteomic batches and ADNI Elecsys data release. Longitudinal models used participant-specific fixed intercepts; comparisons accounted for paired assay estimates and fixed-scale uncertainty. These are pointwise intervals for exploratory candidate comparisons. Complete results are provided in Supplementary Tables S7–S8, S10–S12 and Supplementary Figs. S6, S8–S9; conditional power is shown in Supplementary Fig. S10.

Abbreviations: ADNI-EF, ADNI executive function composite; ADNI-MEM, ADNI memory composite; APOE, apolipoprotein E gene; BY, Benjamini–Yekutieli; CI, confidence interval; CSF, cerebrospinal fluid; p-tau181, tau phosphorylated at threonine 181; SD, standard deviation; TMT-MS, tandem mass tag mass spectrometry.
