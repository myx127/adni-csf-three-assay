**Supplementary Figure S5. Sensitivity of shared protein–p-tau181 associations to APOE and education adjustment.**

Covariate sensitivity was evaluated for all 34 proteins in the primary analysis. The primary model adjusted for age, sex, NULISA plate, SomaScan plate, TMT plex and ADNI Elecsys data release. Sensitivity models additionally included APOE ε4 carrier status, or APOE ε4 carrier status and years of education. All three models used identical participants within each protein and across the three assays; protein-specific sample sizes ranged from 343 to 560. The global standardization of log₂ CSF p-tau181 was held constant across models.

(A) The heatmap shows shared-association q values for the primary model and the two covariate sensitivity models. Numbers are q values rounded to three significant figures; color indicates minus the base-10 logarithm of q, without truncation. Each column retains its original Benjamini–Yekutieli (BY) correction across all 34 proteins. Purple borders identify q ≤ 0.05. Protein names are bold for the 14 proteins meeting this criterion in the primary model; the same 14 proteins met the criterion after each additional adjustment, with positive associations in all three assays.

The intersection–union test evaluated positive associations in all three assays or negative associations in all three, allowing for both directions and using unscaled coefficients. An additional sensitivity analysis jointly corrected the 34 shared-association and 34 relative-response tests across 68 comparisons (Supplementary Table S6).

(B–D) Scatterplots compare primary-model coefficients with the corresponding coefficients after additional adjustment in NULISA, SomaScan and TMT-MS, respectively. Each symbol represents the same protein and assay evaluated in the primary and sensitivity models. Open green circles denote APOE adjustment; purple triangles denote APOE and education adjustment. Each comparison includes all 34 proteins, and similar estimates may overlap. The dashed diagonal denotes identical standardized coefficients. Horizontal and vertical limits match within each panel, while the ranges differ between assays.

Coefficients represent the change in model-specific protein residual standard deviations (SDs) per one global SD of log₂ p-tau181. Therefore, both the regression coefficient and the estimated residual SD can contribute to changes in the plotted standardized estimates. These scatterplots describe the sensitivity of coefficient estimates to adjustment. Full coefficients and pointwise 95% confidence intervals, including residual-SD uncertainty, are provided in Tables S5–S6 and the supplementary data.

MAPT was evaluated separately. Further details are provided in Supplementary Methods Sections 3 and 5.

Abbreviations: APOE, apolipoprotein E gene; BY, Benjamini–Yekutieli; CSF, cerebrospinal fluid; p-tau181, tau phosphorylated at threonine 181; SD, standard deviation; TMT-MS, tandem mass tag mass spectrometry.
