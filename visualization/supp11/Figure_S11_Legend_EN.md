**Supplementary Figure S11. Memory associations under alternative p-tau181 and background adjustment models.**

Panels A–C show NPTX2 (n=553), TAFA5 (n=557) and TIMP3 (n=552), respectively. These candidates were selected using between-assay differences in the preceding analysis of memory trajectories. This exploratory analysis reused the same memory outcome. Within each protein, all nine models retained identical participants, cognitive observations, time-information weights and fixed protein standard deviations (SDs). Eligible ADNI-MEM follow-up assessments were within five years after CSF collection; baseline could precede collection by up to 90 days.

All models included participant-specific fixed intercepts and time interactions with age, sex, education, APOE ε4 carrier status, NULISA plate, SomaScan plate, TMT plex and ADNI Elecsys data release. The row groups distinguish models with no background scores, SomaScan and TMT-MS scores, or all three assay scores. The p-tau181 term followed the row labels: absent, linear on the log₂ scale, linear on the untransformed scale, or a restricted cubic spline of log₂ p-tau181. Spline knots were fixed at the 5th, 35th, 65th and 95th percentiles in the original 560-participant reference sample. All additional terms interacted with time.

Each background score was a participant’s median across robustly standardized reference-protein measurements. The reference sets contained 49 NULISA, 6,001 SomaScan and 1,906 TMT-MS features and excluded all 34 study targets and MAPT, APP and APOE. These relative background scores summarize broad proteomic variation with biological and technical sources.

Circles, squares and diamonds represent NULISA, SomaScan and TMT-MS, respectively. Coefficients are annual ADNI-MEM score change per one fixed assay-specific protein residual SD. All panels use the same horizontal scale and retain every interval. Positive coefficients indicate more favorable memory change, including less decline. Horizontal bars are pointwise 95% confidence intervals (CIs), incorporating coefficient and protein-SD estimation uncertainty. Variances summed the three proteomic technical-cluster contributions with finite-cluster corrections. Dashed lines mark zero association.

Colored cells show BY-adjusted q values for the joint comparison of assay coefficients. The two-degree-of-freedom test evaluated the standardized SomaScan minus NULISA and TMT-MS minus NULISA contrasts together. Correction across 33 additional comparisons included the 27 model comparisons shown here and six background-matched paired-change tests in Supplementary Table S15. Colors represent −log₁₀q, rose borders mark q≤0.05 and values are rounded to three significant figures. Results with correction across all 42 adjustment comparisons are provided in Supplementary Table S14. These candidate analyses were exploratory.

All 27 model comparisons met calibration criteria under the simulated conditions. Direct paired changes after p-tau181 adjustment are reported in Supplementary Table S15. Model definitions and full results are provided in Supplementary Table S14 and Supplementary Methods Sections 6–7, Table M1 and Appendices A, B and D.

Abbreviations: ADNI-MEM, Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative memory composite; APOE, apolipoprotein E gene; BY, Benjamini–Yekutieli; CI, confidence interval; CSF, cerebrospinal fluid; SD, standard deviation; TMT-MS, tandem mass tag mass spectrometry.
