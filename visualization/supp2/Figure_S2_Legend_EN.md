**Supplementary Figure S2. Coverage and technical characteristics of CSF protein measurements across three assays.**

Measurement coverage and technical variation were characterized in 571 participants with paired CSF measurements. Protein-level panels include the 43 targets that could be uniquely matched across assays, before the subsequent quality screening described in Supplementary Fig. S1.

(A) Each point represents one protein. Measurement availability is the percentage of the 571 paired participants with a usable result: a reported NPQ value for NULISA, a valid positive RFU for SomaScan, and valid positive sample and global internal standard (GIS) intensities for TMT-MS. NULISA values below the limit of detection (LOD) were retained. Boxes show the median and interquartile range (IQR), and whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5 IQR; all protein points are shown.

(B) The histogram shows the percentage of participants with a detectable NULISA measurement for each protein. Detectability was defined by NPQ at or above the corresponding target- and plate-specific LOD. The dashed line marks the 80% criterion. Eligibility for pathology analyses required this criterion in both the paired cohort and the analysis sample for each protein. Seven of the 43 proteins were below 80% in the paired cohort.

(C) Each point represents the number of paired participants in a technical batch: 19 NULISA plates, 36 SomaScan plates and 65 TMT plexes. Each assay’s allocation includes all 571 participants. Boxes and whiskers are defined as in Panel A.

(D) Each point represents one protein. The percentage of protein variation explained by TMT plex (R²) was compared before and after GIS normalization, using identical participants with usable GIS measurements for each protein. Before normalization, measurements were log₂-transformed sample intensities; after normalization, they were log₂ sample-to-GIS intensity ratios. R² describes variation explained by TMT plex alone. The dashed diagonal denotes unchanged R². Median R² decreased from 77.3% to 15.9%. S100A12 was the only protein with higher R² after normalization and is labeled.

Measurement and preprocessing details are provided in Supplementary Methods Section 2 and Supplementary Tables S2–S3. Complete measurement summaries are included in the supplementary data.

Abbreviations: ADNI, Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative; CSF, cerebrospinal fluid; GIS, global internal standard; IQR, interquartile range; LOD, limit of detection; NPQ, NULISA Protein Quantification; RFU, relative fluorescence units; TMT-MS, tandem mass tag mass spectrometry.
