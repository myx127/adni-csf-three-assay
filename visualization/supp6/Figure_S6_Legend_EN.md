**Supplementary Figure S6. Associations of CSF proteins with annual memory change across three assays.**

All 34 proteins in the primary analysis are shown alphabetically. Protein-specific sample sizes ranged from 342 to 557 participants, as shown in the n column. The three assay models used identical participants and cognitive observations within each protein. Memory change was estimated from the baseline ADNI-MEM assessment and eligible follow-up assessments within five years after CSF collection. Baseline could precede CSF collection by up to 90 days, with follow-up extending up to five years after CSF collection. Actual follow-up characteristics are shown in Supplementary Fig. S3.

(A–C) Symbols show the estimated annual memory-score association for NULISA, SomaScan and TMT-MS, respectively, with pointwise 95% confidence intervals (CIs). Displayed coefficients are ADNI-MEM score units per year per one fixed assay-specific protein residual standard deviation (SD). Positive coefficients indicate a more favorable annual memory change, including less decline. Models included participant-specific fixed intercepts and time interactions with age, sex, education, APOE ε4 carrier status, standardized log₂ CSF p-tau181, all three proteomic technical factors and ADNI Elecsys data release. The scaling regressions excluded p-tau181 and cognitive scores; the fixed protein SDs were held fixed across clinical analyses. CIs account for uncertainty in the coefficient and protein scaling, using the three proteomic batch contributions to covariance. All three panels use the same horizontal scale; gray dashed lines denote zero association.

(D) Blue circles show evidence for memory associations in the same direction across assays; coral diamonds show evidence for differences between assays. Positions represent −log₁₀ of BY-adjusted q, and the dashed line marks q=0.05. The intersection–union test evaluated unscaled coefficients in both possible directions. A two-degree-of-freedom paired Wald test jointly evaluated the standardized SomaScan minus NULISA and TMT-MS minus NULISA contrasts, accounting for paired measurements and scale uncertainty. BY correction covered 68 primary comparisons: two tests for each of 34 proteins. Filled symbols indicate q≤0.05. No same-direction memory association met the corrected threshold. NPTX2, TAFA5 and TIMP3 showed between-assay differences; correction across all 204 cognitive comparisons retained NPTX2 and TAFA5 (Supplementary Table S7).

Bold names and pale blue shading identify the 14 proteins with shared p-tau181 associations in Supplementary Fig. S4. Memory testing and correction included all 34 proteins.

†CST3 did not meet calibration criteria for memory inference. Its coefficients and pointwise intervals are shown descriptively in gray, and Panel D uses a dash for unavailable inference. Both CST3 hypotheses remained among the 68 comparisons, with P=1 assigned for multiplicity correction.

Further details and complete results are provided in Supplementary Methods Sections 3, 4 and 6 and Appendix A, Supplementary Table S7, and the supplementary data.

Abbreviations: ADNI-MEM, Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative memory composite; APOE, apolipoprotein E gene; BY, Benjamini–Yekutieli; CI, confidence interval; CSF, cerebrospinal fluid; SD, standard deviation; TMT-MS, tandem mass tag mass spectrometry.
